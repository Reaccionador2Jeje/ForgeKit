# AtDev Billing — Hybrid plans (ForgeKit)

**Status:** proposal only. ForgeKit's open-source toolkit and local CLI remain free.

## Hybrid model

Keep local commands, core system utilities, and self-hosting free. Potential paid services may include optional hosted fleet management, signed update distribution, managed policy dashboards, or team support after those services exist. Never restrict access to a user's own local machine or essential security information.

## Proposed tiers (CLP/month; exploratory)

| Plan | Reference price | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Community | $0 | Local toolkit and self-hosted use |
| Supporter | Voluntary | Optional contribution; no core feature restrictions |
| Team | $1,990 | Future hosted team management and policy reporting |

Do not advertise unavailable features. Any hosted control plane must use least-privilege credentials, auditable actions, tenant isolation, and clear data export/deletion.

No real-money checkout is active. Review operational costs and obtain adult account-owner review of payment-provider eligibility and legal/tax obligations before launch.
